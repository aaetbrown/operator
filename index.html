Be the Grid Operator

Keep the lights on for a region, one power plant at a time.

How to play
  • Real grid operators make sure the electricity being made (supply) matches the electricity being used (demand) at every moment.
  • For each time of day, set how many megawatts (MW) each power plant makes. Your supply must equal the demand.
  • Turn on the cheapest plants first. Fill a plant up before you move to the next one.
  • The price is the cost of the most expensive plant you had to use.
  • Press Check my grid when you think you are done. You can change your answer and check again.

Emergency actions

When there is not enough supply, operators have a few more tools. Use them wisely.
